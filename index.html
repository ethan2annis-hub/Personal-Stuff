The Real Trade-Off

Keep the property: You get $1,702/month average cash flow to spend on life (vacations, savings, whatever) for the next 15 years. That's $306,000 in your pocket. The property ends at $782K.

Sell the property: You get zero monthly cash flow, but end with $811K in liquid stocks. That's $29K more at the end, but you miss out on 15 years of supplemental income.

Keeping the property generates $277,000 MORE total value, but requires work. Selling gives you simplicity and a slightly bigger lump sum at the end.

The Bottom Line

Choose "Keep & Self-Manage" if: You value the $1,702/month cash flow and don't mind being a landlord. You'll generate the most total value among safe options ($1.09M) and have money to spend along the way. This is the conservative winner.

Choose "Sell & Invest (Stocks)" if: You want simplicity, liquidity, and zero landlord work. You'll have $811K in liquid stocks at the end, fully accessible whenever you need it. If stocks beat 10.07% annually, this wins.

Choose "Keep & Hire PM" if: You love owning real estate but hate the work. You'll still beat selling by $201K in total value, with minimal effort required. Best of both worlds at a cost.

Choose "Bitcoin" if: You're a degen who can handle seeing -50% swings without panic selling, believe in hyperbitcoinization, and can afford to lose it all. Could turn $256K into $2M-7M+ (or $0). Your wife better be on board because this is a wild ride. 🚀⚠️

Most people should choose: Keep & Self-Manage (safest high-value option) or Sell & Invest in Stocks (simplest). Only go Bitcoin if you're truly built different.
